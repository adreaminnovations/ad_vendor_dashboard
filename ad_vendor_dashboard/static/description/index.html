Vendor Dashboard

Real-time Insights & Performance Tracking

Module Overview

The Vendor Dashboard module empowers your vendors with a dedicated, real-time interface to track their performance, manage products, and monitor orders. By providing a visual overview of key metrics, it enhances transparency and collaboration between your company and your suppliers.

Key Features & Benefits

  • Performance Metrics: Visual charts and graphs for sales, delivery performance, and quality ratings.
  • Order Tracking: Real-time status updates on purchase orders, shipments, and payments.
  • Product Management: Quick access for vendors to manage their product listings and stock levels.
  • Notifications: Alerts for new orders, pending approvals, and important updates.
  • User-Friendly Interface: Clean and intuitive dashboard designed for ease of use on any device.

How to Use / Workflow

  1. Install the Vendor Dashboard module.
  2. Grant dashboard access to your approved vendors.
  3. Vendors log in to their portal to view the dashboard.
  4. They can monitor their performance stats, view open orders, and update product details.
  5. Use the insights to improve vendor relationships and supply chain efficiency.
